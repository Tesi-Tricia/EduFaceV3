Accuracy and bias test plan

Nothing here has been run yet. The numbers are targets. The face engine (a small open-source model running in your browser) has not been tested on your students, cameras or lighting.

Why it matters

Face recognition works unevenly across skin tones, ages, genders, lighting and cameras. In a school this can mean some children are wrongly marked absent — or, worse, marked as someone else. Risk is higher for children (faces change fast) and in poor light. This engine is also a lighter, older model than the server edition's, so test it honestly.

How to test

Volunteers

  • Only with written consent (guardian for children). Treat the test like real enrolment. Tell volunteers the data is deleted afterwards.
  • At least 20 per group (more is better; with 20 people one error is 5%, so treat results as a warning, not proof).
  • Groups: skin tone (light / medium / dark), age (under 12 / 12–14 / 15–18 / adult), gender, glasses, headscarves, braids or hair over the forehead, school caps, siblings/twins (hardest for FAR). Record anonymous group labels, not names.

Conditions (each volunteer)

Morning sun on the face · backlit · overcast/shade · indoor classroom light · dim. Camera at chest vs eye height; distance 0.5 m / 1 m / 2 m.

Procedure

  1. Enrol each volunteer normally (3–5 frames).
  2. Genuine attempts: each volunteer scans 5 times per condition at the real gate camera. Note: recognised / unknown / not sure / wrong person, and time.
  3. Impostor attempts: people who are not enrolled (staff, older volunteers, siblings of enrolled students) scan. Any "matched" is a false accept. Aim for 500+ impostor attempts overall.
  4. Repeat after 2–3 months: children's faces change. Decide how often to re-enrol (suggested: yearly, sooner for under-12s).

Tuning (only after measuring)

  • Maximum distance (Settings → Face recognition; default 0.50): lower = stricter (fewer wrong accepts, more rejects). Change in steps of 0.02–0.05 and re-run the impostor test.
  • Gap to next-best student (default 0.08): raise it for schools with many siblings.
  • Improve the data before the thresholds: better light, camera height, 5 frames at different angles, re-enrol poor ones.
  • Never loosen a threshold to make one group pass if it raises wrong accepts.

Photos and phone screens

There is no anti-spoofing in this edition. A printed photo or a phone screen can fool the scan. Treat face scan as a convenience for attendance, not identity proof. Never use it to release a child to an adult.

Report and follow-up

Write one page: groups, conditions, FRR/FAR table, decisions (thresholds, who uses QR instead), date, who ran it. Keep it with the privacy assessment. Repeat after any change of camera, location or threshold, and yearly. Weekly in use: if one class suddenly has many "not recognised", suspect light or the camera, not the children.

Ethics: volunteers can withdraw anytime and have their test data deleted. Report by group only, never by individual child.
