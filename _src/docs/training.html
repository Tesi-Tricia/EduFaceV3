Staff training guide

No computer skills needed beyond using a tablet or PC. Practise on a training copy (Settings → Load demo data, on a spare browser profile), never on the real school.

Everyone (10 minutes)

What EduFace does: at the gate the camera recognises a student's face (or reads their QR card) and marks them present or late. It also records visitors. It works without internet.

  1. Consent first. A child's face is scanned only if the parent/guardian signed the consent form. No consent = QR card or manual mark. Never pressure a parent.
  2. Be fair. If the camera does not recognise a child, it is the machine's mistake. Use the QR card or a manual mark. Never punish.
  3. Keep your password private. Never share logins. Sign out when you leave. After 15 minutes of no activity you are signed out automatically (the gate kiosk is the exception).
  4. Everything is recorded. Changes you make are logged with your name and a reason.
  5. No photos or screenshots of student lists on personal phones.

Teachers

See who is in class

Attendance → choose the date and class → Present / Late / Absent / Excused for each child.

Correct a mistake

  1. Attendance → find the child → Override.
  2. Choose the status and direction.
  3. Write the reason (required, e.g. "arrived with mother at 8:40").
  4. Save. Your name and the reason are recorded.

A child is not recognised

Stay calm: (1) look at the camera, step into the light; (2) still failing → scan the QR card; (3) no card → Override; (4) tell the administrator if it happens daily (re-enrol the face).

Security / front desk

  • Check in a visitor: Visitors → name, phone, ID number, person visiting, purpose → Check in → print the badge. If it says BLOCKED, do not admit: call the head teacher/security lead. A watch warning means admit only as instructed and inform the lead.
  • Check out: scan the badge QR or press Check out. A red overstay label means: go and check on them.
  • Emergency roll call: Roll call lists everyone on campus now (students last scanned in + visitors). Print it and take it to the assembly point. Also count classes by hand.

School administrator

  • Add students: Students → + Add, or Template → fill in a spreadsheet → Import CSV. Print QR cards.
  • Enrol faces: Enrol faces → choose the student → record the guardian's consent (you must hold the signed form) → Start camera → capture 3–5 frames following the hints (Move closer, Too dark, Face the camera) → Finish.
  • Withdrawn consent: Enrol faces → student → Withdraw consent and delete face data. Done immediately; the child uses the QR card.
  • Daily: after the gate closes, Attendance → Close day marks children with no scan absent (and queues guardian messages).
  • Weekly: Backup → Download backup. Keep the file and passphrase apart.
  • Monthly: Audit log → check it says Chain intact; remove accounts of leavers; check consents.

When things go wrong

SituationDo this
Power cutPaper registers. Start the computer again; open the app; your data is still there.
Camera does not startThe address must be http://localhost:8080. Allow the camera when asked. Close other programs using it (Teams, Zoom).
Face engine "preparing"Wait up to a minute after signing in. Use QR cards meanwhile.
Child not recognisedLight/angle → QR card → manual override with reason.
Parent asks to delete their child's dataWithdraw consent (above). Same day. Tell the administrator.
Forgot the administrator passwordIt cannot be recovered. Restore from a backup made when you still knew it.
Wrong person markedOverride with reason; tell the administrator (two children may look alike).

Practice test (sign-off)

  • ☐ Sign in and out; change language.
  • ☐ (Teacher) override an absent child to late with a reason; run and export a late-comers report.
  • ☐ (Security) check in a visitor, print the badge, check out by QR; try a blocked visitor; open and print the roll call.
  • ☐ (Admin) import 3 students; record consent and enrol a face; withdraw consent and see the face data disappear; make and restore a backup.
  • ☐ Everyone: say the three rules — consent first, be fair, keep passwords private.
